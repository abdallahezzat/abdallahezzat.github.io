About Me

I'm Abdallah Ezzat. I have a huge passion for teaching, especially when it comes to helping students grasp challenging physics concepts. From an early stage in my academic career, I have enjoyed the challenge of making abstract ideas — which often require a great deal of imagination — more accessible to those who struggle with them.

As a student researcher, I love exploring complex data and innovating solutions. With experience in several programming languages, including Python, C++, and FORTRAN, I've played a role in the ATLAS and FASER experiments at CERN's Large Hadron Collider in Geneva, Switzerland.
